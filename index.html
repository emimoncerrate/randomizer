      How Class Roulette works

      Switch classes with the pills up top — each of your six classes keeps its own roster, spin history and progress.

      • Spin the wheel (or press Space) to call on a random student.
      • Once picked, a student sits out until everyone in the class has had a turn — then the round resets automatically.
      • Edit roster to add, remove or paste in a full class list.
      • Undo last pick fixes a misclick or a student who stepped out.
      • Everything is saved in this browser, so it's ready to go next class period.
